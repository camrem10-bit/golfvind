How it works

My Caddie helps you choose a club based on the distance to your target, wind direction and speed, and whether the green is uphill or downhill.

  1. Open Edit: My bag. Check the prefilled carry and roll distances and adjust them to match your clubs.
  2. Open Wind and tap Use my location. Tap Enable live direction the first time you use this feature. Then point the top of your phone towards the target. The app detects your shot direction and estimates how the wind affects your club choice.
  3. Slope Compensation: Enter the elevation difference to the green if it is uphill or downhill.
  4. Add to Home Screen: Open the app link in Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share button and select Add to Home Screen. You can then open My Caddie directly from your Home Screen.

Distances are estimates for straight shots. Wind on the course may differ from the forecast because of trees, terrain and gusts.
